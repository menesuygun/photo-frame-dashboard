Data sources

Football

Scores, standings, fixtures, player leaders, team logos are supplied by ESPN. Turkish headlines are provided through Google Haberler, linking to the original publishers. Kickoff times are displayed in America/Toronto time. Updates depend on the provider; delayed data is identified when available.
